Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough, and there are no online trophies — but there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. The story takes around 60 hours; the platinum takes around 200 hours at difficulty 4/10. Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude, a Chapter 1 meditation spot that disappears after that chapter.

Before you start

  • No mission replay, no New Game+. Free roam carries on after the story, so almost everything can be finished afterwards — but anything tied to a story moment that passes cannot be replayed. Save at every chapter start.
  • Cheats disable trophies — and saving. Turning on a cheat switches trophies off, and you cannot save again once you have used one. Cheats only unlock after the story, so keep them off your trophy save entirely.
  • 100% completion (“A Long Road”) is the hardest trophy. Its checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) shows up after the story; everything you did before already counts, and nothing on it is missable.
  • 28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until you earn them. The spoiler setting above hides their names, descriptions and guides until earned.
